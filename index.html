Ingest: SD Card Photo Import — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Ingest: SD Card Photo Import ("the app") is published by Lee Consult LLC. Contact: leeconsultllc@gmail.com.

Your photos. The app reads photos and videos on your device, and on an SD card you choose, only to display them and to copy the ones you select into Pictures/ImageGalleryPro on your phone. Originals on the card are never modified. Photos, videos, file names, paths and EXIF metadata are not uploaded.

Usage statistics. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how importing and the upgrade are used. It sends app events (for example, a card was scanned, an import started or finished, the upgrade screen was shown), with counts rounded into ranges, the number of free imports left and, for purchases, the price or an error code. Firebase also records automatic events such as first open, session start and in-app purchase, together with an app instance identifier, device and operating system details and an approximate location derived from your IP address. The advertising ID is not collected and ad personalisation is off. You can turn usage statistics off in the app; this stops collection and resets the analytics data held on the device. Google processes this data on our behalf: Firebase privacy information. Data is encrypted in transit.

Purchases. The app is free with a limited number of imports and offers a one-time Pro unlock. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment details.

Sharing. We do not sell your data and there are no ads.

Local storage. An on-device database records which files were imported. It is deleted when you uninstall or clear app data.

Deletion requests. Email leeconsultllc@gmail.com to ask about or request deletion of analytics data.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.
