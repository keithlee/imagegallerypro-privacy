ImageGalleryPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

ImageGalleryPro ("the app") is published by Lee Consult LLC. Contact: leeconsultllc@gmail.com.

What the app does with your data. The app reads photos and videos on your device, and on an SD card you choose, only to display them and to copy photos you select into Pictures/ImageGalleryPro on your phone. Originals on the card are never modified.

What we collect. Nothing. The app has no account system, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no network permission. Your photos, their metadata (including EXIF) and your import history never leave your device. We do not share data with third parties.

Permissions. Photos/videos access is used to show your gallery. Notifications and a foreground service show import progress. Folder access (chosen by you) lets the app read the SD card.

Local storage. An on-device database records which files were imported so they are skipped next time. It is deleted when you uninstall or clear app data.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.
